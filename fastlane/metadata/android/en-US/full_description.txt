Stow is an offline packing checklist built around a personal catalogue of the things you
actually own.

Quantities come from explicit rules resolved against trip length: socks are one per night
plus a spare, capped at ten; a passport is one. Items move through three states, not two —
"to buy" is a pre-trip errand, not an unpacked item, and it never counts towards progress.

A second mode covers the other half of the problem. As often as you forget to pack
something, you leave it behind: a charger in the socket behind the bed, laundry on the
balcony, food in the fridge. The return list tracks what came home and remembers what
didn't, so the charger you keep forgetting eventually says so.

Trips export as PDFs whose checkboxes are real form fields, so you can send one to whoever
you are travelling with and they can tick it in any reader.

The app has no network capability at all — the merged manifest contains no permissions,
and the build fails if one appears.
